# Revenue Concentration

Measure dependence on major revenue sources and translate it into financial risk.

## Workflow
1. Aggregate trailing-12-month and current-period revenue by customer, product, channel, and location where relevant.
2. Calculate top-1, top-5, and top-10 concentration percentages.
3. Compare concentration over time and identify growing dependencies.
4. Review gross margin, contract term, churn/renewal risk, receivable exposure, and strategic importance for major concentrations.
5. Stress-test loss or reduction of major customers and quantify revenue, EBITDA, and cash impact.
6. Separate related parties and one-time projects from recurring diversified revenue.

## Output
Show concentration table, trend, high-risk dependencies, downside scenarios, and recommended mitigation such as diversification, contract renewal, pricing, credit protection, or pipeline development.
