# Business Combinations
Identify acquirer, acquisition date, consideration, contingent consideration, acquired assets/liabilities, working capital, debt-like items, identifiable intangibles, deferred taxes, noncontrolling interests, transaction costs, and measurement-period items. Build opening balance sheet and goodwill bridge.

## Guardrails
Purchase-price allocations and fair-value measurements should be reviewed by qualified accounting/valuation professionals.
