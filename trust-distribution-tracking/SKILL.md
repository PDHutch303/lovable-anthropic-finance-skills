# Trust Distribution Tracking
Record beneficiary, amount, date, cash/noncash form, purpose, approval, source account, and governing provision if known. Tie distributions to bank/custody records and trust accounting. Track YTD totals and potential DNI/K-1 implications as tax-review items.
