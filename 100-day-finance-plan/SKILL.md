# 100-Day Finance Plan
Sequence Day 1 controls, cash visibility, payroll/AP continuity, opening balance sheet, 13-week cash flow, close calendar, KPI baseline, budget/forecast, working-capital actions, systems cleanup, control remediation, and board reporting. Assign owner, deadline, dependency, and success metric to each action.
