# Unfunded Commitment Tracking
Track original commitment, amendments, cumulative funded capital, recallable distributions if applicable, remaining commitment, investment period, and expected call timing. Aggregate by entity and period. Compare expected calls with available liquidity and planned distributions. Flag clustered calls and liquidity shortfalls.
