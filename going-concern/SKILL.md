# Going Concern
Assess cash, forecasted operations, borrowing availability, maturities, covenant compliance, capital commitments, contingent obligations, customer/vendor dependence, and management mitigation plans over the relevant assessment period.

## Output
Provide liquidity forecast, adverse conditions, mitigation plans, sensitivity analysis, evidence gaps, and disclosure/review considerations.

## Guardrails
Do not issue an audit conclusion; this workflow supports management and auditor analysis.
