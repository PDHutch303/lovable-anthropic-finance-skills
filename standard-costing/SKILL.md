# Standard Costing
For each SKU define standard material quantities/prices, labor routing and rates, machine time, variable/fixed overhead, and landed-cost assumptions. Document effective dates and approval. Compare standards to recent actual purchasing and production data. Avoid resetting standards merely to eliminate unfavorable variances.
