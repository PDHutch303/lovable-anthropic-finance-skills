# Job Profitability
Tie contract/job revenue to labor, materials, subcontractors, equipment, permits, freight, commissions, warranty, and other direct costs. Show original estimate, approved changes, actual-to-date, estimate-to-complete, forecast margin, and final margin. Flag unbilled work, missing costs, overruns, and jobs with deteriorating margin.
