# Fraud Risk
Review incentives/pressures, opportunities, rationalization indicators, management override, unusual journal entries, related parties, duplicate/round-dollar payments, vendor changes, payroll anomalies, revenue cutoff, manual adjustments, and unsupported transactions.

## Output
Provide red flags, evidence needed, control gaps, recommended investigation steps, and escalation items.

## Guardrails
Do not accuse individuals of fraud without substantiated evidence.
