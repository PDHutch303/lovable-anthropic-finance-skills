# Family Office Liquidity
Build a 13-week and 12-24 month liquidity view across entities.

Include cash by entity, restricted cash, expected inflows, operating needs, tax payments, debt service, capital calls, planned investments, distributions, insurance, major personal obligations when in scope, and minimum reserves. Identify transfer restrictions and intercompany consequences before recommending cash movement.

## Output
Show liquidity by entity, consolidated economic liquidity, funding gaps, timing risks, and recommended sources of liquidity.

## Guardrails
Do not assume cash is freely transferable among entities or trusts.
