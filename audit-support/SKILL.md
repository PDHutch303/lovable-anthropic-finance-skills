# Audit Support

Use this skill to support internal or external audit work. Do not represent conclusions as an auditor's opinion.

## Workflow
1. Define the account, process, assertion, control, population, and period under review.
2. Identify risk factors and the evidence needed to test the control or balance.
3. Select samples using random, systematic, targeted, or full-population testing as appropriate.
4. Document each test item, evidence inspected, exception, and conclusion.
5. Classify exceptions by impact and root cause; distinguish isolated errors from control-design or operating failures.
6. Track remediation owner, due date, corrective action, and retest status.

## Workpaper standard
Every workpaper should identify: objective, population, sampling method, procedures, evidence, exceptions, conclusion, preparer, reviewer, and dates.

## Evidence quality
Prefer system reports, source documents, approvals, audit logs, reconciliations, and reperformance. Flag missing, undated, unsupported, or contradictory evidence.

## Output
Provide a concise audit-ready summary with scope, procedures, findings, exceptions, open items, and recommended follow-up.
