# Accounting & Close Operating Skill

Apply `QUALITY-STANDARD.md` to every material or review-facing task.

## Operating order
1. Pin entity, period, accounting basis, currency, data-as-of and materiality.
2. Identify the source set and authoritative evidence.
3. Reconcile/tie out balances before interpretation.
4. Use the applicable bundled playbook.
5. Surface unresolved differences and missing evidence.
6. Separate proposed entries from approved/posted entries.
7. Deliver review-ready schedules, conclusions and next actions.

## Route by task
Use the bundled playbooks for onboarding/setup, chart of accounts, receipt/invoice extraction, transaction categorization, bank and balance-sheet reconciliation, cleanup/catch-up, accruals/prepaids, journal entries, payroll, inventory/COGS, fixed assets, AP/AR, payments, billing, close management, controller review, financial statements, variance analysis, dashboards, multi-entity/intercompany/consolidation, revenue recognition, leases, related parties, business combinations, debt/equity accounting, accounting policies, GAAP disclosures and technical-accounting research.

Use the QuickBooks Online, Xero and Stripe playbooks only when those systems are relevant. Never claim live system access unless an actual connection confirms it.

## Core controls
- Never use a balancing plug merely to finish a reconciliation or close.
- Bank, card, AR, AP, payroll, inventory, fixed-asset and other material subledgers must tie to the GL or remain an explicit exception.
- Do not infer accounting substance from the direction of a cash transfer alone.
- Preserve legal-entity boundaries and mirror intercompany treatment before elimination.
- Historical actuals are source records, not forecast assumptions.
- Accounting standards and filing/reporting requirements that may have changed require current authoritative guidance.

## Deliverable
State scope, source set, tie-out status, exceptions, proposed entries/reclasses, financial-statement impact, review required and next actions.
