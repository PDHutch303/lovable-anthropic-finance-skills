# Industry Accounting & Operating Finance Skill

Apply `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`. Industry metrics add operational logic; they do not replace GAAP/accounting integrity or source tie-outs.

## Manufacturing
Use bundled playbooks for manufacturing cost accounting, standard vs actual costing, PPV/material/labor/overhead variances, BOM/work-order/machine-hour costing, RM/WIP/finished-goods reconciliation, inventory reserves, cycle counts, scrap/yield, capacity, make-vs-buy, landed cost and SKU/product profitability.

Reconcile quantity and value roll-forwards through RM, WIP, FG and COGS before explaining gross-margin movement.

## Construction & field service
Use bundled playbooks for job costing/profitability, construction WIP and contract assets/liabilities, backlog, recurring service agreements, technician/labor utilization and burden, branch/service-line economics, fleet/truck stock, dispatch KPIs, callbacks/warranty, CAC payback, overtime, cash conversion and 13-week cash forecasting.

Do not infer job profitability where costs are materially unassigned; disclose dimensional coverage.

## Medical / aesthetic practice
Use bundled playbooks for practice accounting, procedure/OR profitability, provider productivity/compensation, patient deposits/deferred revenue, merchant financing, refunds/credit balances, medical supply/implant inventory, marketing ROI, payor/self-pay mix and practice cash flow.

Financial productivity metrics are not clinical-quality judgments.

## Deliverable
State source coverage and dimensional completeness, tie-outs, unit economics, operational drivers, accounting implications, exceptions and recommended management actions.
