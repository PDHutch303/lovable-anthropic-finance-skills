# Family Office & Private Equity Skill

Apply `QUALITY-STANDARD.md` and preserve legal entity, trust, fund and beneficial-owner boundaries.

## Operating order
1. Pin owner/entity/fund/trust, as-of date, currency and source vintage.
2. Distinguish legal ownership, accounting carrying value, tax basis/capital, manager-reported NAV/fair value and internal estimated value.
3. Reconcile beginning balances plus activity to ending balances before reporting performance.
4. Track contributions, distributions, fees, income, realized/unrealized changes, commitments and capital accounts separately.
5. Surface stale valuations, missing statements/K-1s and unresolved NAV/basis breaks.
6. Verify changed wire instructions independently; never initiate payment solely from a notice/email.
7. Identify tax, legal, estate/trust, investment and valuation review points.

## Route by task
Use bundled playbooks for family-office consolidation/reporting/liquidity/cash forecasting; private investment and fund roll-forwards; NAV reconciliation; capital calls/distributions and notice parsing; capital-account, basis, K-1 and unfunded-commitment tracking; IRR/MOIC; waterfalls/preferred returns/carry; entity maps; portfolio-company reporting; custody aggregation; IPS/portfolio reporting/drift; financial planning and wealth diagnostics; trusts, estates, beneficiaries and distributions; philanthropy; succession; and family-council records.

## Core controls
- Do not force an unresolved NAV difference through valuation.
- Do not mix tax capital/basis with book/NAV value.
- Do not treat internal aggregation as changing legal ownership.
- Do not present planning analysis as guaranteed returns or unauthorized individualized trade instructions.

## Deliverable
Provide the roll-forward/tie-out, source dates, exceptions, liquidity/commitment exposure, performance calculations where relevant, legal/tax/value distinctions, approvals/professional review and next actions.
