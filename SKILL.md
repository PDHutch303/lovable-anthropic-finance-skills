# CFO, FP&A, Modeling & M&A Skill

Apply `QUALITY-STANDARD.md` before material analysis.

## Operating order
1. Define the decision, entity/perimeter, period, data-as-of and source model/data.
2. Tie historical actuals and key schedules before forecasting or underwriting.
3. Separate facts, management assumptions and analyst assumptions.
4. Use one controlled calculation/model version across deliverables.
5. Run downside/scenario tests and identify decision thresholds.
6. Surface unresolved diligence, covenant, liquidity and accounting issues.
7. Present decision options, not manufactured certainty.

## Route by task
Use bundled playbooks for strategic finance, budgeting, rolling forecasts, scenarios, 13-week/longer cash forecasts, working capital, profitability, concentration, runway, treasury, debt/covenants, capital allocation/structure, valuation, investment analysis, three-statement modeling, model audit/debugging, M&A analysis, LBO modeling, quality of earnings, sources & uses, IC memos, purchase-price allocation, opening balance sheets, working-capital true-ups, seller notes, earnouts, transaction costs, goodwill/intangibles, rollover equity, post-close integration, 100-day finance plans and synergy tracking.

## Model controls
- Historical statements must tie to source.
- Cash cannot be a hidden plug.
- Sources = uses; debt schedules = balance sheet; cash flow = ending cash.
- Returns must emerge from assumptions rather than target-seeking a desired IRR.
- QoE accounting corrections are distinct from EBITDA normalization.
- Unresolved diligence remains visible in IC and transaction recommendations.

## Deliverable
Show the decision requested, evidence/source set, model version, assumptions, base/upside/downside, key sensitivities, liquidity/leverage, exceptions, accounting/tax implications where relevant, approvals and next actions.
