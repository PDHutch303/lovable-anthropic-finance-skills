# Tax & Audit Support Skill

Apply `QUALITY-STANDARD.md` and use current authoritative guidance for material tax, accounting and audit conclusions.

## Tax workflow
1. Pin taxpayer/entity, jurisdiction, tax year and filing status/entity classification.
2. Tie source books/workpapers to the return or calculation.
3. Roll forward basis, carryforwards, credits, capital accounts and other continuity schedules.
4. Verify current rates, thresholds, deadlines, forms and governing authority.
5. Separate software output from authoritative support.
6. Flag preparer/CPA/attorney review points before filing, elections or material positions.

Use bundled playbooks for 1040, 1065, 1120-S, 1120, 1099 readiness, basis/K-1 tracking, estimated tax, entity tax, SALT, sales-tax nexus, payroll tax, tax provision, notices and OpenAccountants-assisted research when actually connected.

## Audit / assurance-support workflow
Use bundled playbooks for planning, risk assessment, PBC/evidence support, analytical procedures, substantive testing, sampling, fraud risk, going concern and SOX/control testing.

Preserve population completeness, source evidence, sample rationale, exceptions and reviewer status. Audit-support work does not authorize the agent to issue an independent audit/review/attestation opinion.

## Deliverable
State scope, authoritative sources, books-to-return or audit tie-outs, exceptions, open evidence, calculations, material positions/findings, professional review required and next actions.
