# Make vs Buy
Compare avoidable/incremental internal cost with supplier landed cost. Include capacity opportunity cost, tooling/capex, quality, scrap, lead time, logistics, minimum buys, IP/supply-chain risk, and working capital. Separate sunk/allocated costs from decision-relevant cash flows.
