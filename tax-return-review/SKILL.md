# Tax Return Review

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`, especially the current-authority rule.

## Preflight
Confirm taxpayer/entity, return type, tax year, filing jurisdictions, draft version/date, accounting basis, final books/workpapers, prior-year return, ownership, elections, and known extensions/payments.

## Books-to-return reconciliation
Tie material return lines to final books, tax workpapers and source forms. Reconcile book income to taxable income and identify permanent/temporary differences, tax-only adjustments, nondeductible items and separately stated items as applicable.

## Continuity review
Compare to prior return and workpapers for:
- entity/taxpayer information and ownership;
- elections and accounting methods;
- depreciation/amortization continuity;
- NOL, credit and other carryforwards;
- shareholder/partner/member basis and at-risk/passive items where applicable;
- capital accounts and debt allocations;
- estimated/extension payments and prior overpayments;
- state apportionment/nexus and filing footprint;
- foreign/information-reporting items where relevant.

## Diagnostic review
Investigate large year-over-year changes, return lines inconsistent with books/source forms, negative or impossible balances, missing K-1/1099/W-2/1098 data, ownership percentages that do not reconcile, Schedule L/M reconciliation problems, and positions lacking support.

## Authority and software
Tax software diagnostics are useful controls but are not legal authority. For material/uncertain treatment, identify tax year/jurisdiction and verify current primary authority or trusted professional research before concluding.

## Output
Provide:
- return version and scope;
- books-to-return tie-out status;
- prior-year continuity issues;
- source forms/payments reconciled;
- open questions/missing documents;
- potentially material tax positions requiring preparer review;
- state/local/information-return gaps;
- filing-readiness verdict: ready for preparer final review / ready with items / not ready.

## Guardrails
Do not file, sign, make elections, or represent a position as final solely from this review. Do not invent thresholds, rates, deadlines or tax rules from memory when they are material.
