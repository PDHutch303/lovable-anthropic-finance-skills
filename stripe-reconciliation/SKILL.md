# Stripe Reconciliation

Reconcile Stripe activity at gross transaction level and payout level. Separate charges, taxes, refunds, disputes, fees, reserves, and payout timing. Match customer receipts to invoices or revenue records before clearing to cash. Tie Stripe balance activity to bank deposits and the GL. Identify missing payouts, duplicate revenue, unapplied receipts, fee misclassification, refund/chargeback differences, and cutoff issues. Output gross activity, net payouts, fees/adjustments, timing differences, GL tie-out, and proposed correcting entries.
