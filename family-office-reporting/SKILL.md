# Family Office Reporting
Create a consolidated economic view while preserving legal-entity boundaries.

## Workflow
1. Inventory entities, trusts, accounts, investments, debt, guarantees, and ownership.
2. Reconcile entity-level financials before consolidation.
3. Separate operating businesses, real estate, marketable securities, private investments, cash, debt, and personal-use assets.
4. Report NAV, liquidity, income, cash flow, commitments, unfunded obligations, debt, taxes, and distributions.
5. Track intercompany balances and ownership changes separately from economic performance.
6. Highlight concentration, liquidity, tax, and governance risks.

## Output
Provide family balance sheet/NAV, liquidity schedule, investment roll-forward, debt summary, commitment schedule, tax calendar, and key actions.

## Guardrails
Do not collapse trust, estate, tax, or legal ownership distinctions into a single economic balance without disclosure.
