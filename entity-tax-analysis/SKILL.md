# Entity Tax Analysis
Map legal entity, tax classification, ownership, compensation, distributions, retained earnings, state footprint, debt, planned exit, and intercompany activity. Compare entity-level tax, owner-level tax, payroll/self-employment considerations, distribution constraints, basis, compliance, and transaction consequences.

## Output
Provide comparison matrix, assumptions, major advantages/disadvantages, cash-flow impact, and issues for tax/legal advisors.

## Guardrails
Do not recommend an entity conversion without modeling transaction taxes, elections, state effects, and legal implications.
