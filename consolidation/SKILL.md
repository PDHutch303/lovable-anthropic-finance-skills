# Consolidation

Confirm reporting entities, ownership, reporting period, accounting basis, and account mappings. Reconcile each entity before consolidation. Eliminate intercompany receivables/payables, revenue/expense, loans, interest, management fees, and intercompany profit where required. Confirm equity roll-forward and treatment of noncontrolling interests when applicable. Check that eliminations net to zero and do not alter consolidated external cash. Output entity results, elimination entries, consolidated balances, and unresolved differences.
