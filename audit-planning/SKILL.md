# Audit Planning
Define scope, reporting period, entities, material accounts, significant transactions, deadlines, audit contacts, PBC requirements, and prior-year findings. Map each requested item to owner, source system, reviewer, status, and due date.

## Output
Provide audit calendar, PBC tracker, account/evidence map, open issues, and escalation list.
