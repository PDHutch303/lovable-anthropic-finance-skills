# Personal Financial Statement
Organize assets, liabilities, contingent liabilities, guarantees, ownership interests, income sources, and liquidity as of a stated date. Avoid double-counting assets held through entities. Distinguish gross asset value from debt and net equity.

## Output
Provide assets, liabilities, net worth, liquidity, contingent obligations, valuation basis, and documentation gaps.

## Guardrails
Clearly label estimated or non-marketable values and avoid presenting the statement as audited.
