# Technical Accounting Research
1. State the accounting question and complete fact pattern.
2. Identify authoritative guidance and effective date.
3. Separate recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, and transition issues.
4. Analyze alternatives and judgments.
5. Document conclusion, entry impact, disclosures, controls, and required professional review.

## Output
Provide issue, facts, guidance, analysis, conclusion, entries, disclosures, and open questions.

## Guardrails
Use current authoritative sources for consequential conclusions and distinguish research support from an audit opinion.
