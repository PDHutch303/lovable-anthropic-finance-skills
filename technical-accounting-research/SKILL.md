# Technical Accounting Research

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md` and verify current authoritative literature.

## Scope
Pin entity, reporting framework (for example US GAAP), reporting period, transaction/facts, materiality, question presented, and any existing policy/precedent.

## Research hierarchy
Prefer applicable authoritative accounting literature and official standard-setter material. Use firm guides, articles and examples as secondary interpretive support, not as a substitute for the governing literature.

## Workflow
1. State the accounting question narrowly.
2. Separate verified facts from assumptions and missing facts.
3. Identify relevant codification/standard topics and effective dates.
4. Summarize the recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure requirements relevant to the facts.
5. Apply each material criterion to the facts; do not jump directly from topic name to conclusion.
6. Analyze reasonable alternatives and why they are accepted/rejected.
7. Quantify financial-statement impact and transition/entry mechanics where possible.
8. Identify disclosures, controls and future-period consequences.
9. Record source references and research date.

## Output
Use memo structure: issue, facts, assumptions, guidance, analysis, alternatives, conclusion, entries/financial impact, disclosures, open items, reviewer approval.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent paragraph citations.
- Do not rely on superseded guidance.
- If facts are insufficient, state a provisional conclusion or decision tree rather than asserting certainty.
- Material or unusual conclusions should identify the qualified-review point.
