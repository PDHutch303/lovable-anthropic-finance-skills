# Medical Practice Accounting
Reconcile patient receipts, financing/merchant settlements, insurance where applicable, deposits, refunds, provider compensation, payroll, medical supplies, implants, facility/OR costs, marketing, and location results. Separate clinical revenue from product/retail and membership/package activity. Maintain HIPAA/privacy boundaries and avoid clinical judgments.
