# Bookkeeping Setup
Confirm entity, tax year, accounting basis, reporting needs, bank/credit accounts, payroll, sales channels, inventory, fixed assets, loans, owners, intercompany activity, and accounting platform. Build chart of accounts, opening balances, bank feeds, close procedures, document storage, approval controls, and recurring reconciliation schedule.

## Output
Provide setup checklist, chart-of-accounts recommendations, opening-balance plan, integrations, monthly close calendar, and unresolved items.
