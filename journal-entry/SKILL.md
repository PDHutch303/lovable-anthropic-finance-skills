# Journal Entry

Use this skill to draft and review journal entries after the supporting calculation is understood.

## Debit-credit logic
- Assets and expenses normally increase with debits and decrease with credits.
- Liabilities, equity, and revenue normally increase with credits and decrease with debits.
- Contra accounts should be evaluated according to their function rather than blindly applying the normal balance of the related account.

## Workflow
1. State what economic event occurred.
2. Identify affected accounts and entities.
3. Determine the correct debit and credit for each line.
4. Confirm total debits equal total credits.
5. Add a clear memo explaining the business purpose.
6. Confirm posting period and cutoff.
7. Identify whether reversal or intercompany elimination is required.
8. Require reviewer approval for material, unusual, manual, related-party, or estimate-based entries.

## Review checks
Check mathematical accuracy, source support, account classification, entity coding, duplication, sign direction, cutoff, tax implications, and consistency with prior-period treatment.

## Output
Present the proposed entry in a simple table with account, debit, credit, entity/dimension, and explanation, followed by assumptions and required approvals.
