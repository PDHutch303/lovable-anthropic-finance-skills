# Invoice Aging

Segment open receivables by customer and aging bucket. Identify overdue, high-value, disputed, repeatedly late, credit-risk, and unapplied-cash items. Tie the aging total to the AR control account. Track last contact, promised payment date, dispute reason, owner, and next action. Distinguish true delinquency from billing errors, credits, unapplied cash, or timing issues. Output prioritized collection actions, aging summary, concentration risk, and items needing accounting adjustment or reserve review.
