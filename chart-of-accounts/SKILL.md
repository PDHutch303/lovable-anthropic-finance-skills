# Chart of Accounts
Design accounts around financial-statement presentation, material business drivers, tax needs, entity structure, and reporting dimensions. Minimize duplicate or overly granular accounts; prefer classes, departments, locations, customers, or projects when dimensional reporting is better than new GL accounts.

## Output
Provide proposed account number/name/type, parent grouping, purpose, examples, mapping from legacy accounts, and accounts to merge or inactivate.

## Guardrails
Preserve historical traceability and do not delete accounts with prior activity.
