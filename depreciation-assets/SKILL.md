# Depreciation & Fixed Assets

Confirm entity, asset description, in-service date, cost, capitalization policy, class, useful life, method, salvage value, and tax/book basis when relevant. Distinguish repairs and maintenance from capital expenditures. Roll forward beginning cost and accumulated depreciation through additions, disposals, transfers, depreciation, and impairment. Tie the fixed-asset register to the GL. Prepare proposed depreciation, disposal, transfer, and reclassification entries. Flag unsupported assets, fully depreciated assets still in service, missing disposals, unusual useful lives, and book/tax differences for review.
