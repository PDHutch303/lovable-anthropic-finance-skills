# Profitability Analysis

Determine where the business actually earns or loses money.

## Workflow
1. Define the profitability object: product, job, customer, location, business unit, channel, or service line.
2. Map revenue and directly attributable costs first.
3. Separate direct costs, variable overhead, fixed overhead, and corporate/shared costs.
4. Calculate gross margin, contribution margin, and fully allocated operating profit where appropriate.
5. Use a rational allocation basis for shared costs and clearly label allocated versus directly traced costs.
6. Compare current period, prior period, budget, and trend.
7. Decompose changes into price, volume, mix, labor/productivity, material/input cost, and overhead effects.
8. Identify negative-margin or low-return segments and test whether they are strategically necessary.

## Output
Rank profitability by segment, show margin percentages and dollars, explain major drivers, disclose allocation assumptions, and identify concrete actions such as repricing, sourcing changes, labor changes, mix shifts, or discontinuation.
