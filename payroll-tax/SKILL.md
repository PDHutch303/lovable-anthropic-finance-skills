# Payroll Tax
Reconcile payroll registers to GL, cash, Forms 941/940, W-2/W-3 inputs, state/local payroll filings, tax deposits, benefits, and employer taxes. Track jurisdictions, filing frequencies, notices, amendments, and unresolved variances.

## Guardrails
Verify current filing/deposit requirements and do not infer worker classification from payroll treatment alone.
