# Strategic Finance
Build a decision-oriented financial view of the business.

## Workflow
1. Define the decision, time horizon, and success metric.
2. Reconcile the historical baseline before forecasting.
3. Identify key value drivers: revenue, gross margin, labor, working capital, capex, debt, tax, and cash conversion.
4. Build base, downside, and upside cases with explicit assumptions.
5. Quantify P&L, balance-sheet, cash-flow, liquidity, and covenant impact.
6. Identify leading indicators, decision triggers, and management actions.
7. Separate controllable operating actions from external assumptions.

## Output
Provide the strategic question, key assumptions, quantified scenarios, major risks, recommended actions, and monitoring KPIs.

## Guardrails
Do not treat forecasts as facts. Preserve actuals and identify assumptions, confidence level, and sensitivity to key drivers.
