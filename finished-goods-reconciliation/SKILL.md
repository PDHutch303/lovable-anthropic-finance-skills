# Finished Goods Reconciliation
Roll forward beginning FG + completions/transfers - shipments/COGS - scrap/write-offs +/- adjustments = ending FG. Tie units to inventory records and dollars to valuation. Review negative inventory, unusual adjustments, cutoff, and margin anomalies.
