# Family Office Private Investment Accounting

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`.

## Set up the investment record
Capture legal investor/entity, investment/fund/SPV, commitment date/amount, ownership/security, currency, accounting/tax classification, manager/custodian, valuation policy, governing documents and source IDs.

Keep separate ledgers for:
- legal/accounting carrying value;
- tax basis/capital where relevant;
- commitment/unfunded amount;
- performance cash flows;
- manager-reported NAV/valuation.

## Transaction workflow
For every capital call/distribution/fee/transfer:
1. parse notice and effective/due date;
2. reconcile to commitment and prior activity;
3. classify contribution, management fee/expense, return of capital, income, realized gain, withholding, recallable distribution or other component using source support;
4. verify cash independently after settlement;
5. update carrying value/basis/commitment schedules under the applicable policy;
6. retain source notice and approval.

Changed wire instructions require independent verification; a notice/email alone is not sufficient.

## Period-end roll-forward
Beginning carrying value + contributions + recognized income/gains/valuation change - distributions/return of capital/fees as applicable = ending carrying value. Reconcile ending NAV/capital to manager statement and explain book-tax-NAV differences rather than choosing one number arbitrarily.

## Performance
Calculate IRR/MOIC/DPI/RVPI/TVPI only from clearly defined cash-flow conventions and valuation date. Show whether returns are gross/net and before/after fees when data supports it.

## Output
Provide investment roll-forward, commitment/unfunded schedule, distribution classification, NAV/basis reconciliation, performance metrics with definitions, stale/missing data, and entries/review items.

## Guardrails
Do not treat NAV as audited fair value merely because a manager reports it; do not infer tax basis from GAAP/book carrying value; do not initiate capital-call payments.
