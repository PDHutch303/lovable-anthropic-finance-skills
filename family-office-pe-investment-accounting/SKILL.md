# Family Office PE Investment Accounting
Identify investor entity, ownership, tax classification, commitment, funded capital, and reporting currency. Roll forward beginning investment + calls/contributions + allocated income/loss + supported valuation changes - distributions - write-offs = ending investment. Separate return of capital, income, realized gain/loss, unrealized change, fees, and carry effects when supported. Tie capital statements, K-1 inputs, wires, and GL balances. Track unfunded commitments separately. Do not infer fair value, tax basis, or distribution character without support.
