# Capital Allocation
Evaluate competing uses of capital on a risk-adjusted, liquidity-aware basis.

## Workflow
1. Establish available cash, minimum liquidity reserve, borrowing capacity, and constraints.
2. List candidate uses of capital and required timing.
3. Estimate expected return, payback period, downside loss, cash-flow profile, and strategic value.
4. Compare against cost of capital and alternative uses.
5. Model effects on leverage, DSCR, working capital, and liquidity.
6. Rank opportunities by risk-adjusted return and strategic fit.
7. Define approval thresholds and post-investment review metrics.

## Output
Provide a capital-allocation table, recommendation, expected return, downside case, liquidity effect, and decision triggers.

## Guardrails
Do not recommend deploying cash needed for taxes, payroll, debt service, or required operating reserves.
