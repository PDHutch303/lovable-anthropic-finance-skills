# Landed Cost
Tie purchase invoices to freight, duty/tariff, brokerage, insurance, and other eligible acquisition costs. Define allocation basis by shipment/SKU. Reconcile landed-cost accruals and true-ups. Keep period expense and inventory-capitalizable items distinct according to applicable accounting policy.
