# SOX / Internal Control Testing

Document the risk, assertion, control owner, frequency, control description, population, and testing objective. Evaluate design before operating effectiveness. Select samples appropriate to control frequency and risk. Inspect evidence, document exceptions, and distinguish isolated execution failures from design deficiencies. Consider compensating controls and aggregate related deficiencies. Track remediation owner, due date, corrective action, and retest. Do not characterize a deficiency as a formal significant deficiency or material weakness without qualified professional review.
