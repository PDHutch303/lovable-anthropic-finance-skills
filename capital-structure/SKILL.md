# Capital Structure
Design and compare financing structures based on cost, control, liquidity, and risk.

## Workflow
1. Map existing debt, equity, maturities, rates, covenants, guarantees, and liens.
2. Determine funding need and repayment source.
3. Compare debt, preferred, common equity, seller financing, and hybrid structures.
4. Model interest, amortization, dilution, cash sweep, DSCR, leverage, and covenant headroom.
5. Stress downside cash flow and refinancing assumptions.
6. Identify control rights, collateral exposure, and liquidity consequences.

## Output
Provide structure alternatives, cash-flow effects, ownership/dilution, covenant impact, and recommended parameters.

## Guardrails
Separate financial modeling from legal or securities-law conclusions.
