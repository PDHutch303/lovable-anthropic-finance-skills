# QuickBooks Online

Use this skill for QBO-oriented accounting support. Confirm entity/company file and reporting period before proposing changes.

## Workflows
- Customers and invoices: review customer setup, invoice status, credits, unapplied payments, and AR aging.
- Vendors and expenses: review vendor setup, bills, expenses, payment status, coding, classes/locations, and AP aging.
- Payments: reconcile deposits, undeposited funds, processor settlements, fees, refunds, and unapplied cash.
- Reports: use P&L, balance sheet, trial balance, general ledger, AR/AP aging, transaction detail, and reconciliation reports as appropriate.
- Reconciliation: tie bank/credit-card accounts and material balance-sheet accounts to supporting detail.

## Controls
Avoid duplicate transactions, uncategorized or suspense balances, stale undeposited funds, one-sided intercompany entries, and unsupported journal entries. Preserve audit trail and document proposed changes.

## Output
State the QBO report/data needed, proposed bookkeeping action, accounting rationale, and validation step after entry.
