# Related Party Review
Identify owners, affiliates, common-control entities, family relationships when relevant and provided, loans, management fees, rent, reimbursements, purchases/sales, guarantees, and distributions. Reconcile both sides and evaluate presentation/disclosure requirements.

## Guardrails
Do not assume arm's-length terms or tax treatment without evidence.
