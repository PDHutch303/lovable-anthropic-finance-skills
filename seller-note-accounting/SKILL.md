# Seller Note Accounting
Document face amount, issue date, maturity, stated rate, payment schedule, contingencies/subordination, and any required discount/premium analysis. Build principal/interest amortization and reconcile payments. Separate acquisition consideration from subsequent interest expense and obtain technical/tax review for unusual terms.
