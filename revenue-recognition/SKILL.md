# Revenue Recognition

Use this skill to prepare revenue-recognition support, not to make unsupported GAAP conclusions. Identify contract/customer, promised goods or services, pricing, billing terms, delivery or service period, acceptance terms, variable consideration, and modifications. Compare invoicing to performance and distinguish recognized revenue, contract assets, receivables, and deferred revenue. Review cutoff around period end and reconcile deferred-revenue roll-forwards to the GL. Flag unusual terms, bundled arrangements, rebates, refunds, modifications, or judgments for qualified accounting review. Output contract summary, recognition schedule, proposed entries, and open technical questions.
