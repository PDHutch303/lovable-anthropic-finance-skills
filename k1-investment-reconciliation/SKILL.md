# K-1 Investment Reconciliation
Match entity/EIN, ownership, capital, contributions, distributions, income categories, gains/losses, liabilities, credits, state schedules, and footnotes to internal records. Explain differences among tax capital, book NAV, and cash. Maintain an unresolved-items log for amended or incomplete K-1 data.
