# Entity Structure Mapping
Create an entity master with legal name, jurisdiction, entity type, tax classification, owners and percentages, parent/subsidiary relationships, EIN/account identifiers where provided, bank accounts, debt, guarantees, key agreements, accounting system, tax return, responsible professionals, and intercompany relationships.

## Output
Provide an ownership tree, entity matrix, missing-information list, and recurring compliance/reporting calendar.

## Guardrails
Label unknown ownership or tax classifications as unresolved rather than inferring them.
