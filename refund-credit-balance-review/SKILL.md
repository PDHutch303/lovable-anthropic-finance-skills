# Refund & Credit Balance Review
Age patient credit balances and unapplied receipts. Trace to original payment, procedure, financing source, refund policy, and subsequent service. Identify duplicate payments, canceled procedures, chargebacks, and credits requiring action. Do not direct refunds without authorization and identity/payment verification.
