# Wealth Diagnostic Memo
Build a consolidated economic balance sheet while preserving legal ownership. Summarize assets, liabilities, liquidity, cash-flow needs, investment concentration, private commitments, tax exposures, insurance gaps, guarantees, entity complexity, trust/estate administration, philanthropy, and succession issues. Separate facts from assumptions and rank issues by urgency, magnitude, reversibility, and professional owner. Output: current-state snapshot, key risks, missing information, 90-day administrative actions, and topics for CPA/attorney/investment-adviser/insurance review. Do not characterize the memo as legal, tax, appraisal, or individualized securities advice.
