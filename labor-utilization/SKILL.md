# Labor Utilization
Reconcile paid hours to productive/billable hours and classify travel, training, PTO, idle, shop, and overtime. Calculate utilization and revenue per paid hour. Trend by technician, crew, branch, service line, and period while distinguishing capacity issues from demand or scheduling issues.
