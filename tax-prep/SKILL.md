# Tax Preparation Support

Use this skill to prepare clean books and organized workpapers for the tax preparer; do not determine uncertain tax positions without qualified review.

## Workflow
1. Confirm entity type, tax year, accounting method, and ownership changes.
2. Reconcile cash, debt, AR/AP, payroll liabilities, sales tax, fixed assets, equity, and intercompany balances.
3. Review uncategorized, suspense, meals, travel, auto, home-office, charitable, legal, penalties, owner/shareholder, and related-party accounts.
4. Prepare fixed-asset additions/disposals and book depreciation detail.
5. Prepare 1099/W-9 exception list and payroll/W-2 tie-outs.
6. Identify nondeductible, potentially capitalizable, estimated, or unusual items for tax-professional review.
7. Tie the final trial balance and financial statements to the tax workpapers.

## Output
Provide a tax-prep checklist, reconciled balances, proposed book adjustments, missing documents, and open tax questions.
