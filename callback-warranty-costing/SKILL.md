# Callback & Warranty Costing
Identify repeat visits/warranty jobs linked to original work. Capture labor, materials, truck cost, credits/refunds, lost capacity, and reason code. Calculate callback rate and cost by technician, product, job type, vendor, and branch. Separate normal warranty reserve from identifiable quality problems.
