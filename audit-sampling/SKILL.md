# Audit Sampling
Define the complete population, sampling unit, objective, assertion, risk, tolerable error/deviation, selection method, and treatment of exceptions. Use random/systematic selection for representative testing and targeted selections for specifically identified risks; do not represent judgmental selections as statistically representative.

## Output
Provide population, method, sample, rationale, results, exceptions, and conclusion.
