# Partnership Waterfalls
Read the governing agreement first. Build tiers in contractual order: return of capital, preferred return/hurdle, catch-up, promote/carry, residual sharing. Identify deal-by-deal versus whole-fund structure when determinable. Show distributions by tier and remaining balances. Do not invent legal interpretations when language is ambiguous.
