# Scenario Planning
Build decision-ready scenarios with explicit drivers and triggers.

## Workflow
Define the decision and horizon; establish a reconciled base case; vary a limited set of material drivers; quantify P&L, balance sheet, cash, liquidity, leverage, and covenant effects; identify breakpoints and management actions; assign leading indicators and trigger thresholds.

## Output
Show assumptions, scenario results, sensitivities, key risks, and action plan.

## Guardrails
Do not hide assumptions inside formulas or present scenarios as forecasts with certainty.
