# Contractor & 1099 Review

Use this skill for bookkeeping preparation, not tax advice. Build a year-to-date vendor payment list, identify potentially reportable vendors, confirm W-9/name/TIN/entity-type information, separate payments made through reportable versus potentially excluded payment channels, and flag missing or inconsistent data. Reconcile totals to the GL and vendor detail. Output vendors requiring review, missing W-9s, potential reporting form category, payment totals, and unresolved exceptions for tax-professional confirmation.
