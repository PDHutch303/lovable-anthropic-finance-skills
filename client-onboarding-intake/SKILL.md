# Client Onboarding Intake
Collect legal entity name, EIN/tax classification, ownership, fiscal year, accounting basis, reporting requirements, materiality, locations, currencies, bank/card/loan accounts, payroll, merchant processors, AP/AR systems, inventory/fixed assets, related parties, tax jurisdictions, prior returns, prior financials, opening balances, key contracts, and close cadence. Map systems and source-of-truth owners. Validate beginning equity and balance-sheet opening balances. Produce missing-document list, risk list, proposed COA/reporting architecture, recurring close calendar, and responsibility matrix. Keep each legal entity’s context isolated unless consolidation is explicitly required.
