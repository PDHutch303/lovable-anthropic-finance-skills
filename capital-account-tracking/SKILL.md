# Capital Account Tracking
Maintain beginning capital + contributions + allocated income/gain - allocated loss/deductions - distributions +/- transfers = ending capital. Keep book capital, tax capital, outside basis, and ownership percentage separate. Reconcile partner statements/K-1 schedules to the GL and governing documents. Flag negative capital, disproportionate distributions, and unexplained changes.
