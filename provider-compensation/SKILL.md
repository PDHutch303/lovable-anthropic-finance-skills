# Provider Compensation
Read employment/contract terms first. Reconcile compensation to the agreed base: collections, production, RVUs, procedures, contribution, or other metric. Include thresholds, exclusions, clawbacks, benefits, and timing. Flag discrepancies between payroll and contract calculations and avoid legal interpretation when terms are ambiguous.
