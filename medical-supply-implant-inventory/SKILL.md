# Medical Supply & Implant Inventory
Track purchases, receipts, lot/serial data where available, usage by procedure, returns, waste, expirations, and ending inventory. Reconcile high-value implants/injectables to procedures and patient billing. Flag expired, slow-moving, missing, or unusually high usage. Preserve clinical traceability requirements.
