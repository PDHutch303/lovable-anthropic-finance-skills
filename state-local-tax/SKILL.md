# State & Local Tax
Inventory legal entities, employee locations, property, inventory, offices, customers, revenue by jurisdiction, registrations, filing history, and notices. Identify potential income/franchise, sales/use, payroll, gross-receipts, and property-tax obligations for professional review.

## Output
