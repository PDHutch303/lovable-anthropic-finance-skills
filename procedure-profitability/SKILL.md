# Procedure Profitability
For each procedure calculate net revenue less surgeon/provider variable compensation, anesthesia/OR/facility cost, implants, drugs/supplies, disposables, financing/merchant fees, commissions, and other attributable variable cost. Show contribution margin before shared overhead and fully loaded margin separately. Segment by provider and location when useful.
