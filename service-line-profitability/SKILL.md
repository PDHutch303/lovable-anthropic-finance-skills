# Service Line Profitability
Segment revenue and direct cost by service line. Include technician labor/burden, materials, truck/fleet cost, commissions, warranties/callbacks, and attributable marketing where support exists. Compare ticket size, gross margin, conversion, repeat work, and seasonality.
