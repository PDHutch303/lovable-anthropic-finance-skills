# Construction Job Costing
Maintain contract value including approved changes, costs to date by cost code, commitments, billed-to-date, cash collected, estimate-to-complete, and forecast margin. Reconcile AP, payroll, POs/subcontracts, and billing to job ledgers. Flag unapproved change orders, missing committed costs, over/underbilling, and margin fade.
