# Compilation & Review Preparation
Finalize reconciliations, support material balances, prepare financial statements and footnote inputs, organize debt/equity/lease/related-party/subsequent-event information, document unusual transactions, and maintain a PBC tracker. For review engagements, prepare analytical explanations and corroborating support.

## Guardrails
Do not describe internal preparation work as a CPA compilation, review, or assurance conclusion.
