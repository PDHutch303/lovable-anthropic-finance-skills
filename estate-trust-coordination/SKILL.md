# Estate & Trust Coordination
Maintain a financial inventory of trusts and estates, trustees, beneficiaries when provided, owned entities/assets, bank and investment accounts, distributions, tax documents, estimated payments, professional contacts, and deadlines.

## Output
Provide trust/estate financial map, cash schedule, distribution ledger, tax-document tracker, and unresolved advisor questions.

## Guardrails
This workflow supports administration; it does not interpret trust instruments or provide estate-planning legal advice.
