# Quality of Earnings

Start from reported financials and reconcile to the underlying GL and supporting schedules. Identify owner-related, nonrecurring, discretionary, transaction-related, unusual, or out-of-period items that may affect normalized earnings. Analyze revenue trends, gross margin, customer concentration, recurring versus project revenue, seasonality, working capital, capex, and cash conversion. Distinguish factual adjustments from judgmental add-backs and require clear support for each. Output reported EBITDA, proposed normalization bridge, recurring earnings assessment, working-capital observations, major risks, and diligence questions.
