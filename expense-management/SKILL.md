# Expense Management

Control and classify operating spend while maintaining sufficient documentation.

## Workflow
1. Require merchant/vendor, date, amount, business purpose, receipt, employee/cardholder, entity, account, department, and project/job where relevant.
2. Match card transactions and reimbursements to receipts and approvals.
3. Flag missing support, duplicate submissions, personal/non-business items, split transactions, unusual merchants, policy exceptions, and sales/use-tax issues.
4. Code expenses consistently to the chart of accounts and dimensions.
5. Separate reimbursable client/job costs from corporate operating expenses.
6. Review recurring subscriptions and vendors for duplicate tools, inactive users, and pricing changes.
7. Accrue material incurred expenses not yet submitted at period end.

## Output
Provide exception list, coding recommendation, missing documentation, approver/owner, policy status, and month-end accounting impact. Do not approve unsupported or apparently personal spend merely because it was paid on a company card.
