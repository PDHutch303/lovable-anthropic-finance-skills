# Treasury & Cash Management

Manage liquidity deliberately while preserving entity-level accounting and control.

## Workflow
1. Prepare daily/weekly cash position by bank account and legal entity.
2. Separate operating cash, restricted cash, reserves, undrawn revolver capacity, and investment balances.
3. Forecast near-term receipts, payroll, AP, taxes, debt service, capex, and owner/investor distributions.
4. Maintain minimum operating cash thresholds by entity.
5. Recommend transfers only after identifying their accounting substance: intercompany loan, contribution, distribution, reimbursement, management fee, or other transaction.
6. Track intercompany cash movements symmetrically and reconcile due-to/due-from balances.
7. Sequence payments based on due date, discount economics, vendor criticality, covenant restrictions, and liquidity.
8. Review excess cash for approved debt reduction or investment options consistent with policy.

## Output
Provide cash position, near-term uses/sources, minimum cash, available liquidity, recommended transfers/payments, accounting treatment, and risks. Never characterize a cash transfer as revenue or expense solely from the movement of cash.
