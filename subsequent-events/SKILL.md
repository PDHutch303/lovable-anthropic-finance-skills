# Subsequent Events
Review significant cash activity, debt changes, litigation/claims information, acquisitions/disposals, casualty events, customer/vendor failures, equity transactions, distributions, covenant events, and other major developments after period end. Determine whether facts relate to conditions existing at the balance-sheet date or arose afterward.

## Output
Provide event log, financial impact, proposed accounting/disclosure treatment, and professional-review items.
