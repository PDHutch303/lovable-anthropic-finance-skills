# Cleanup & Catch-Up Bookkeeping
Work chronologically from the last reliable close. Secure bank/credit statements, prior tax returns, payroll reports, debt statements, AR/AP, fixed assets, equity history, and prior reconciliations. Reconcile cash first, then balance-sheet accounts, then P&L classification. Maintain a correction log with period, issue, entry, support, and tax-return implications.

## Output
Provide status by month/account, unresolved differences, proposed corrections, and a clean handoff close checklist.

## Guardrails
Never force unresolved differences to miscellaneous income/expense.
