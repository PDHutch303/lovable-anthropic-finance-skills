# Inventory & COGS

Confirm entity, location, costing method, inventory source report, and reporting period. Reconcile beginning inventory plus purchases/production less usage/sales/adjustments to ending inventory. For assemblies, analyze component usage rather than relying only on finished-good SKU matches. Tie inventory subledger or valuation reports to the GL. Identify negative inventory, stale or obsolete items, unexpected unit-cost changes, duplicate adjustments, cutoff issues, and unexplained shrinkage. Prepare proposed inventory/COGS adjustments only when supported. Output roll-forward, valuation tie-out, exceptions, and recommended entries.
