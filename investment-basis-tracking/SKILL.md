# Investment Basis Tracking
Maintain beginning basis, contributions, income/gain, loss/deductions, distributions, liabilities where applicable, and ending basis. Track book carrying value separately. Reconcile K-1 inputs and prior-year schedules. Flag potentially basis-limited losses or distributions exceeding basis for tax review.
