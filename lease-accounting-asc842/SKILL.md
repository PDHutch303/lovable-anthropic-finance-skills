# Lease Accounting — ASC 842

Extract lease term, commencement date, fixed payments, renewal/termination options, incentives, prepaid amounts, initial direct costs, and discount-rate inputs. Build the payment schedule and roll forward lease liability and right-of-use asset. Separate operating and finance lease treatment only after the relevant classification inputs are documented. Reconcile schedules to the GL and identify modifications, impairments, early terminations, or missing leases. Output lease abstract, schedule, monthly entry support, reconciliation, and technical questions requiring accounting review.
