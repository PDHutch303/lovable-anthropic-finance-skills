# Runway Analysis

Estimate how long current liquidity supports operations under realistic scenarios.

## Workflow
1. Start with unrestricted cash and available borrowing capacity, shown separately.
2. Calculate recent gross burn, net burn, and operating cash flow trend.
3. Adjust for seasonality, known one-time items, debt service, taxes, capex, distributions, and working-capital changes.
4. Define a minimum operating cash floor rather than assuming cash can reach zero.
5. Build base, downside, and severe-downside monthly cash cases.
6. Identify the first month cash falls below the operating floor or covenant requirement.
7. Quantify financing or cost actions needed to extend runway.

## Output
Show starting liquidity, monthly burn/cash generation, minimum cash, runway in months, projected breach date, key sensitivities, and management actions. Distinguish cash on hand from undrawn debt capacity.
