# Capacity Utilization
Define theoretical, practical, scheduled, and actual capacity by constrained resource. Calculate utilization and throughput. Identify bottlenecks, downtime, setup/changeover, labor shortages, and fixed-cost absorption implications. Use practical rather than purely theoretical capacity for operational decisions.
