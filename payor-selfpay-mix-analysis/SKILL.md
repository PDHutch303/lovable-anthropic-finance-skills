# Payor / Self-Pay Mix Analysis
Segment gross and net revenue, collections, adjustments, fees/discounts, collection cycle, and contribution by payment source. For insurance activity, separate contractual adjustments and denials when relevant. For self-pay/financing, track merchant/financing discount and refund behavior.
