# Cash Flow Forecast

Start with verified opening cash by account and entity. Forecast cash receipts from AR, recurring revenue, asset sales, financing, and other known inflows. Forecast disbursements for AP, payroll, taxes, debt service, capex, owner distributions, and other commitments. Separate committed, expected, and discretionary items. Use realistic collection and payment timing rather than accounting recognition dates. Identify minimum cash, borrowing needs, covenant pressure, and major weekly/monthly swings. Compare forecast to actual and update assumptions. Output ending cash, liquidity headroom, key assumptions, downside risks, and required actions.
