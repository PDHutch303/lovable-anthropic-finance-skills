# AR Collections

Prioritize collections using amount, days overdue, customer history, dispute status, strategic importance, and credit risk. Confirm invoice accuracy and application of credits/cash before outreach. Track contact date, response, promised payment date, dispute, owner, and next action. Escalate materially overdue or repeatedly broken promises according to company policy. Keep communications factual and professional. Output prioritized collection queue, recommended next step, and status summary by customer.
