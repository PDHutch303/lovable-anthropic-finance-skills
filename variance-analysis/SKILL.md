# Variance Analysis

Confirm the comparison basis and materiality thresholds. Calculate dollar, percentage, margin, and basis-point changes where relevant. Break material variances into volume, price/rate, mix, timing, one-time, FX, headcount, productivity, or classification drivers. Distinguish recurring run-rate changes from temporary timing effects. Tie explanations to source data rather than narrative guesswork. Output material variances ranked by impact, quantified drivers, management questions, forecast implications, and recommended follow-up.
