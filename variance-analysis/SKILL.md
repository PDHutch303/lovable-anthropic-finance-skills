# Variance Analysis

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`.

## Frame a comparable analysis
Pin current and comparison periods, basis, entity, currency, materiality and whether either period is partial. Do not compare a partial period to a full period without a clear matched-window analysis or prominent qualification.

## Integrity first
Confirm both sides come from the same accounting basis and source spine. For any bridge, dimensional split or driver table, the components must re-sum to the total variance. Unassigned/untagged activity stays visible.

## Workflow
1. Compute dollar movement for all relevant lines.
2. Compute percent movement only when the denominator is meaningful; suppress or mark not meaningful for zero/near-zero, sign-crossing, stub, or extreme bases.
3. Apply materiality using both size and qualitative relevance.
4. For flagged lines, pull transaction, customer/vendor, location/product/project or operational evidence when available.
5. Classify supported drivers such as volume, price/rate, mix, new/lost activity, run-rate, one-time, timing, FX, reclassification or possible error.
6. Distinguish `where` a variance occurred from `why` it occurred.
7. Identify residual/unexplained variance explicitly.

## Commentary standard
A strong explanation states the amount, comparison, supported driver, evidence, and whether the effect is recurring/one-time/timing where evidence supports that classification. If cause is not proven, call it a hypothesis/question.

## Output
Provide total movement, flagged lines, bridge/driver table, evidence, residual, management commentary, questions, and actions.

## Guardrails
- Account names alone are not evidence of cause.
- Do not allocate untagged costs to fabricate dimensional profitability.
- Do not mix budget and actual definitions without normalization.
- Favorable/unfavorable depends on business meaning, not mathematical sign alone.
