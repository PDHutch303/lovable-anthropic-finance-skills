# GAAP Disclosure Review
Map significant balance-sheet and income-statement areas to required disclosures, accounting policies, commitments/contingencies, debt, leases, revenue, related parties, subsequent events, concentrations, fair value, acquisitions, taxes, and equity as applicable. Tie note amounts to the financial statements and workpapers.

## Output
Provide disclosure checklist, tie-out exceptions, missing facts, inconsistent language, and items requiring current technical-accounting review.

## Guardrails
Verify current disclosure requirements for the reporting framework and period.
