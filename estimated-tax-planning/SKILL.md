# Estimated Tax Planning
Project taxable-income inputs from actuals plus forecast, separately track pass-through income, wages, investment income, gains/losses, major deductions, prior carryforwards when provided, withholding, and payments already made. Compare cash needed under relevant safe-harbor or projection approaches only after verifying current rules.

## Output
Provide projection inputs, estimated payment schedule, cash requirement, sensitivity cases, and questions for tax advisor.

## Guardrails
Tax rates, thresholds, and safe-harbor rules must be verified for the applicable year and jurisdiction.
