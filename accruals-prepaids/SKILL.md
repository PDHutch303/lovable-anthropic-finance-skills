# Accruals & Prepaids
Identify services received but not invoiced, payments benefiting future periods, and recurring amortization schedules. Support each entry with vendor, service period, amount, methodology, reversal policy, and account treatment.

## Output
Provide accrual/prepaid schedule, journal entry, reversal/amortization dates, support, and period-over-period roll-forward.

## Guardrails
Do not use accruals as plugs to achieve a target earnings result.
