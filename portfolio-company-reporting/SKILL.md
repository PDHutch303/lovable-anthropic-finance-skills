# Portfolio Company Reporting
Require actuals, prior period, budget/forecast, cash, debt, working capital, capex, and industry KPIs. Normalize definitions across companies while preserving entity-specific books. Highlight EBITDA bridge, liquidity, covenant headroom, concentration, productivity, capex, and material risks. Produce an owner/board summary and action list.
