# Tax Notice Response Support
Capture agency, notice number, taxpayer/entity, tax type, period, issue, amount, response deadline, prior filings/payments, and requested documents. Reconcile the notice to filed returns and account records, identify factual discrepancies, assemble exhibits, and draft a factual response for professional review.

## Guardrails
Do not concede liability or make procedural/legal representations without authorized advisor review.
