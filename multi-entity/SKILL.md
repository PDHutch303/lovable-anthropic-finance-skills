# Multi-Entity Accounting

Use this skill when transactions span parent, subsidiary, sister, holding, operating, or investment entities.

## Workflow
1. Identify the legal entity that incurred the obligation, received the benefit, owns the asset, or earned the revenue.
2. Avoid posting activity to the wrong entity merely because another entity paid or received the cash.
3. Record intercompany receivable/payable, contribution, distribution, loan, management fee, or reimbursement according to the underlying economics and approved policy.
4. Keep due-to/due-from balances reciprocal by counterparty.
5. Apply shared-cost allocations consistently and retain the allocation basis.
6. Reconcile intercompany balances monthly before consolidation.
7. Flag undocumented transfers, one-sided entries, stale balances, and transactions with tax/legal implications.

## Output
Show entity-by-entity entries, intercompany counterparties, elimination impact, and unresolved differences.
