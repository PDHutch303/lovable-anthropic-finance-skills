# Xero Integration

Confirm organization and reporting period before working with Xero data. Use bank feeds and reconciliation carefully; do not create duplicate spend/receive-money transactions when an invoice or bill already exists. Review invoices, bills, contacts, payments, credit notes, tracking categories, chart-of-accounts mappings, and reconciliation status. Tie Xero reports to supporting schedules and flag unreconciled bank items, suspense balances, stale receivables/payables, and inconsistent tracking categories. Output required Xero data, proposed accounting action, rationale, and validation step.
