# Philanthropic Planning
Track charitable budget, grants, DAF/foundation balances, contributions, appreciated-asset gifts, commitments, recipient organizations, and documentation. Model cash and tax-timing implications only with clearly stated assumptions.

## Output
Provide annual giving plan, commitment schedule, funding sources, documentation checklist, and tax-review items.

## Guardrails
Do not make legal or tax deductibility conclusions without current facts and professional review.
