# Audit Risk Assessment
Evaluate account magnitude, complexity, judgment, transaction volume, related parties, unusual transactions, prior errors, fraud susceptibility, system changes, and control environment. Map risks to assertions and planned evidence or controls.

## Output
Provide risk register, significant accounts, assertions, rationale, control/evidence response, and unresolved risks.

## Guardrails
Risk assessment supports audit preparation and does not replace the auditor's professional judgment.
