# Financial Forecasting

Build a rolling forecast that updates from actual performance and explicit business drivers.

## Workflow
1. Define forecast horizon and monthly/weekly granularity.
2. Start with latest closed actuals; do not overwrite historical periods.
3. Forecast revenue by the best available driver: units, customers, backlog, contracts, price, utilization, locations, or run rate.
4. Forecast COGS and operating expenses using causal drivers where possible rather than flat percentages.
5. Model payroll from headcount, start dates, compensation, taxes, and benefits.
6. Model working capital: DSO, DPO, inventory turns, deferred revenue, and other operating balances.
7. Model capex, depreciation, debt service, interest, taxes, distributions, and financing.
8. Reconcile the three statements and identify liquidity constraints.
9. Produce base, upside, and downside cases and identify the assumptions with the largest impact.

## Output
Show monthly forecast, variance from prior forecast/budget, key assumptions, liquidity outlook, and management actions implied by the forecast.
