# Fund Investment Rollforward
Prepare beginning NAV/carrying value, calls, fees, realized proceeds, allocated income/loss, valuation change, distributions, and ending NAV. Track total commitment, cumulative funded capital, cumulative distributions, and remaining commitment. Reconcile GP statements to cash and GL. Flag stale valuations, unexplained NAV movements, duplicate calls, and distributions lacking character support.
