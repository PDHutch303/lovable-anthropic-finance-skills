# Truck Stock Inventory
Track warehouse-to-truck transfers, purchases, job consumption, returns, scrap, and ending truck stock by technician/vehicle. Reconcile periodic counts to system quantity and job material usage. Flag excessive stock, shrinkage, negative quantities, and material issued without job assignment.
