# Beneficiary Reporting
Present beginning assets, receipts/income, investment changes where appropriate, expenses/fees, taxes, distributions, and ending assets. Reconcile to custody/bank records and include beneficiary distribution schedules. Avoid legal conclusions about entitlement or fiduciary duties.
