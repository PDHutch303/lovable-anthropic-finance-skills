# Analytical Procedures
Build an independent expectation from prior periods, budgets, nonfinancial drivers, ratios, seasonality, and known changes. Define a threshold for investigation, quantify differences, obtain corroborated explanations, and determine whether further testing is needed.

## Output
Provide expectation, actual, variance, threshold, explanation, corroborating evidence, and conclusion.
