# Client Profitability

Analyze each client on economic contribution, not revenue alone.

## Workflow
1. Gather client revenue by period and service/product line.
2. Trace direct costs: materials, subcontractors, commissions, shipping, payment fees, and dedicated labor.
3. Estimate service labor using hours x loaded labor cost when available.
4. Capture support burden, credits, returns, discounts, warranty, and collection costs.
5. Calculate gross profit, contribution margin, margin %, revenue per labor hour, and cash realization.
6. Review DSO, late-payment behavior, disputes, concentration risk, and growth trend.
7. Segment clients into strategic/high-profit, growth, maintain, reprice, remediate, or exit categories.

## Output
Provide a client profitability table, assumptions for allocated costs, top/bottom clients, concentration exposure, pricing opportunities, and recommended actions. Avoid allocating corporate overhead in a way that obscures contribution margin; show direct economics separately from fully allocated economics.
