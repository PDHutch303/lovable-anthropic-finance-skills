# Intercompany Reconciliation

Match both sides of each intercompany relationship by entity, account, date, amount, currency, and transaction type. Identify one-sided entries, timing differences, misclassified transfers, inconsistent management-fee treatment, unreconciled loans, and settlement activity. Require reciprocal balances before consolidation. Prepare proposed correcting entries for each entity and distinguish true economic transactions from items that should eliminate in consolidation. Output a counterparty matrix, differences, aging, proposed corrections, and elimination status.
