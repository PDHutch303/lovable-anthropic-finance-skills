# Reconciliation

For each account, identify the GL balance, supporting source, reconciliation date, preparer, reviewer, and materiality threshold. Tie supporting detail to the GL and classify differences as timing, missing transaction, duplicate, misclassification, valuation, cutoff, stale item, or unexplained variance. Prepare correcting entries only when supported. Age reconciling items and escalate stale or material exceptions. Output beginning balance, activity, ending GL balance, support balance, difference, reconciling items, proposed corrections, and reviewer status.
