# Family Council Minutes
Capture meeting date, attendees, agenda, information reviewed, decisions made, matters deferred, conflicts disclosed, responsible owner, deadline, and required follow-up documents. Distinguish discussion from approved action and quote resolutions only when the approved wording is provided. Maintain a decision log for investments, liquidity, philanthropy, governance, education, distributions, and family-enterprise topics. Flag items requiring attorney, trustee, tax, investment-adviser, or board approval. Do not invent consents, fiduciary findings, legal rights, or binding governance provisions.
