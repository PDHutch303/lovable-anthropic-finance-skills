# Journal Entry Prep

Use this skill to prepare proposed journal entries and their supporting schedules. Do not post an entry without sufficient support and approval.

## Workflow
1. Identify the transaction, entity, period, source documents, and accounting objective.
2. Determine the relevant accounts and whether each is increased or decreased.
3. Build the supporting calculation and tie it to source data.
4. Draft debit and credit lines with clear descriptions, entity/class/location dimensions where relevant, and the posting date.
5. Confirm debits equal credits.
6. Explain the accounting rationale and whether the entry is recurring, reversing, reclassification, accrual, deferral, intercompany, consolidation, or correcting.
7. Flag tax, legal, covenant, related-party, or materiality considerations requiring professional review.

## Entry package
Include: JE number or reference, date, entity, accounts, debits, credits, memo, supporting calculation, source links, preparer, reviewer, reversal date if applicable, and approval status.

## Quality checks
Tie calculations to source records, avoid unsupported plug entries, verify period cutoff, check duplicate postings, and document any estimate or judgment.
