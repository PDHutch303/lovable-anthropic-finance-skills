# Management Dashboard

Create concise dashboards that connect financial results to operating drivers.

## Workflow
1. Define the audience and decision cadence: weekly operating review, monthly close, board, lender, or owner review.
2. Select a small set of decision-useful KPIs rather than reporting every available metric.
3. Include revenue, gross profit/margin, EBITDA or operating income, cash, working capital, AR aging, AP, backlog/pipeline, headcount, and business-specific operating KPIs where relevant.
4. Show current period, budget/forecast, prior period, prior year, and trailing trend where useful.
5. Use consistent definitions and source systems for each KPI.
6. Flag material exceptions and explain drivers, owner, corrective action, and expected resolution date.
7. Reconcile financial KPIs to closed accounting data; clearly label operational estimates or preliminary figures.
8. Keep detailed account-level analysis in supporting schedules rather than overcrowding the executive view.

## Output
Provide an executive scorecard, trend commentary, material variances, liquidity/working-capital highlights, top risks, and prioritized management actions.
