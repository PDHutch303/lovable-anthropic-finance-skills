# Inventory Reserve & Obsolescence
Age inventory by last receipt/use/sale, compare on-hand quantities with forecast demand, open orders, service needs, and disposition history. Identify excess, obsolete, damaged, and superseded items. Calculate proposed reserves transparently and reconcile to prior reserve activity. Technical accounting conclusions require review.
