# Construction WIP Accounting
For each contract show contract value, estimated total cost, gross profit, costs to date, percent complete, earned revenue, billings, over/underbilling, and projected margin. Tie costs and billings to GL. Use the accounting policy and current standards applicable to the engagement; do not assume percentage-of-completion treatment without review.
