# Investment Policy Statement
Document objectives, time horizon, liquidity needs, risk capacity/tolerance, return objective, permitted/prohibited asset classes, concentration limits, leverage policy, rebalancing rules, benchmarks, governance, manager selection, reporting cadence, and exceptions process.

## Guardrails
An IPS sets decision rules; it does not select specific securities or replace fiduciary/legal review.
