# Purchase Price Allocation
Start with total consideration and closing balance sheet. Identify acquired assets/liabilities, working capital, debt-like items, identifiable intangible categories, and residual goodwill. Track valuation-source support and provisional amounts. Do not represent internal estimates as independent valuation conclusions; technical accounting and tax treatment require review.
