# Controller Review

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`.

## Preflight
Confirm entity, period, basis, materiality, close status, latest adjustment timestamp, TB/GL, core reconciliations and comparison data. Review must postdate the latest material book change.

## Review sequence
1. Confirm TB balances and financial statements tie to the same adjusted books.
2. Inspect cash, AR, AP, payroll, inventory/WIP, fixed assets, debt, leases, taxes, equity and intercompany support as applicable.
3. Review suspense/uncategorized accounts, negative assets/liabilities, stale reconciling items, unusual debit/credit signs and unexplained control-account differences.
4. Inspect material/manual/top-side journals for support, preparer/approver, period, accounts and business purpose.
5. Test cutoff around period end for revenue, purchases, payroll, inventory/shipping and major cash activity where relevant.
6. Perform period-over-period/budget analysis and require evidence before assigning causal explanations.
7. Review related-party activity, owner transactions, new debt/equity, acquisitions/disposals and unusual contracts.
8. Evaluate whether outstanding exceptions aggregate to a material or qualitative reporting risk.

## Verdict
Use one of:
- `ready for reporting`;
- `ready with disclosed exceptions`;
- `not ready — corrections/reconciliations required`.

## Output
Provide verdict, tie-out status, material findings, proposed corrections, open exceptions/owners, control observations, required professional research, and reviewer sign-off status.

## Guardrails
Do not call books clean merely because the TB balances. A balanced entry can still be wrong. Do not turn analytical anomalies into accounting adjustments without supporting evidence.
