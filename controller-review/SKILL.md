# Controller Review
Review bank reconciliations, AR/AP, payroll, inventory, fixed assets, debt, prepaid/accruals, intercompany, equity, revenue cut-off, unusual journal entries, flux analysis, and statement consistency. Confirm every material balance has support and ownership.

## Output
Provide ready/not-ready conclusion, review notes, proposed entries, open items, owner, due date, and materiality assessment.

## Guardrails
Do not release final financials while material balance-sheet accounts remain unsupported.
