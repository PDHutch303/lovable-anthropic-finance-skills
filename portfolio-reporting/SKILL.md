# Portfolio Reporting
Reconcile beginning value, contributions, withdrawals, income, fees, realized/unrealized changes, and ending value. Report allocation, concentration, liquidity, commitments, performance when source data supports it, and benchmark comparisons where appropriate. Distinguish time-weighted and money-weighted returns.

## Guardrails
Do not calculate or present performance from incomplete cash-flow data without qualification.
