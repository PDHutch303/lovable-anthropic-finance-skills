# Dispatch KPI Analysis
Measure inbound opportunities, booked calls, dispatches, completion rate, conversion, average ticket, drive time, on-site time, same-day capacity, cancellations, and reschedules. Connect operational metrics to revenue per technician-day and labor utilization. Segment by branch, dispatcher, service line, and daypart.
