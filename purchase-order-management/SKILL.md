# Purchase Order Management

Use POs to control commitments before cash is spent and to improve AP accuracy.

## Workflow
1. Define when a PO is required and establish approval thresholds by dollar amount and department.
2. Require vendor, description, quantity, price, account/cost center, project/job, requester, and approver.
3. Match PO to receipt/service confirmation and vendor invoice before payment.
4. Investigate quantity, price, tax, freight, and duplicate-invoice exceptions.
5. Review open POs at month-end for received-not-invoiced accruals and stale commitments.
6. Close or reduce completed/abandoned POs so commitments remain reliable.
7. Track emergency/non-PO spend separately and report policy exceptions.

## Output
Provide PO status, committed amount, received amount, invoiced amount, remaining commitment, exceptions, accrual recommendation, and required approvals.
