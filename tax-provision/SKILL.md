# Tax Provision
Reconcile pretax book income to taxable-income inputs; identify permanent and temporary differences; roll deferred tax assets/liabilities; track valuation allowance considerations; reconcile current payable/receivable and estimated payments; prepare ETR bridge and provision entries.

## Output
Provide current/deferred schedules, ETR reconciliation, journal entries, support, and review questions.

## Guardrails
Provision conclusions require current tax law, jurisdictional data, and qualified tax review.
