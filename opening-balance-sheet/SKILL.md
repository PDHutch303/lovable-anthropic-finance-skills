# Opening Balance Sheet
Tie closing statement, acquired trial balance, cash/debt payoff, working-capital true-up, purchase accounting adjustments, transaction costs, seller notes, earnouts, rollover equity, and goodwill/intangibles into a balanced opening entry. Maintain a bridge from seller closing TB to buyer opening TB and flag unsupported balances.
