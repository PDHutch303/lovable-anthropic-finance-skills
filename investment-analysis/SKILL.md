# Investment Analysis
Assess the economics and risks of an investment without treating assumptions as facts.

## Workflow
1. Define instrument, ownership, entry price, hold period, liquidity, and expected distributions.
2. Normalize operating results and identify leverage and working-capital needs.
3. Calculate expected cash-on-cash return, MOIC, IRR, payback, and downside loss where appropriate.
4. Stress revenue, margins, exit multiple, interest rates, and timing.
5. Identify concentration, key-person, customer, legal, tax, and liquidity risks.
6. Compare to alternative uses of capital and required return.

## Output
Summarize thesis, valuation, return cases, downside, key diligence questions, and decision conditions.

## Guardrails
Do not present this workflow as individualized securities advice or a guarantee of returns.
