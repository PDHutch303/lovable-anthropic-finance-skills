# Lovable Accounting, CPA, Tax, Audit, Family Office & CFO Skill

A Lovable-compatible **single master skill** with a broad professional-finance playbook library for bookkeeping, accounting, CPA/controller work, tax, audit support, family-office administration, FP&A, treasury, valuation, M&A, investment analysis, and CFO decision support.

## Import into Lovable

Use this public repository URL:

`https://github.com/PDHutch303/lovable-anthropic-finance-skills`

Lovable imports **one skill per GitHub repository**. The required root `SKILL.md` is named `accounting-cfo`; all specialized `SKILL.md` files below are bundled playbooks routed by the master skill.

## Coverage

### Bookkeeping & close
Bookkeeping setup, chart of accounts, bank/credit-card reconciliation, transaction categorization, receipt processing, cleanup/catch-up bookkeeping, accruals/prepaids, month-end close, journal entries, account reconciliations, fixed assets, inventory/COGS, and payroll reconciliation.

### AP, AR & spend
Accounts payable, invoice aging, payment reconciliation, collections, dunning, purchase orders, employee/card expense management, and Stripe reconciliation.

### CPA, controller & financial reporting
Controller review, financial statements, variance analysis, board reporting, management dashboards, technical-accounting research, accounting-policy memos, GAAP disclosure review, revenue recognition, ASC 842 leases, multi-entity accounting, intercompany reconciliation, and consolidations.

### FP&A, advisory & corporate finance
Strategic finance, budgeting, rolling forecasts, scenario planning, cash-flow forecasting, working capital, profitability, client profitability, revenue concentration, runway, debt/covenants, treasury, capital allocation, capital structure, valuation, investment analysis, M&A analysis, and quality of earnings.

### Family office
Family-office consolidated reporting/NAV, private-investment tracking, capital calls and distributions, liquidity planning, entity/ownership mapping, personal financial statements, philanthropic/DAF administration, and estate/trust financial coordination.

### Tax
Tax-prep readiness, tax workpapers, draft-return review, contractor/1099 readiness, basis and K-1 tracking, estimated-tax planning support, entity-tax analysis, state/local tax readiness, sales-tax nexus support, and tax-provision support.

### Audit, assurance & controls
Audit planning, audit risk assessment, PBC/evidence support, substantive testing, analytical procedures, audit sampling, fraud-risk review, going-concern support, and SOX/internal-control testing.

### Accounting systems
QuickBooks Online and Xero accounting workflows.

## Design philosophy

The repository stays compact so Lovable can import it without downloading large upstream monorepositories. The master skill preserves entity boundaries, distinguishes facts from assumptions, requires reconciled support, and routes consequential tax, legal, audit, estate/trust, investment, and technical-accounting judgments to appropriate professional review.
