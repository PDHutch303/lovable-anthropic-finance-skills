# Basis & K-1 Tracking
Track beginning basis information when available, contributions, distributions, purchases/sales, income/loss allocations, debt-basis inputs where applicable, ending balance, K-1 receipt, amended K-1s, and source documents. Keep book carrying value separate from tax basis.

## Output
Provide basis roll-forward, K-1 tracker, missing-document list, and items requiring tax-preparer determination.

## Guardrails
Do not calculate deductible-loss limitations or final basis without the entity-specific tax rules and complete facts.
