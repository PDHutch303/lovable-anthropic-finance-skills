# Payment Reconciliation

Reconcile payments from bank, merchant processor, or payment platform to AR and the GL. Match by customer, invoice, date, amount, batch, and reference. Separate gross receipts, processing fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves, and timing differences. Identify unapplied cash, duplicate applications, short pays, overpayments, missing deposits, and settlement timing items. Tie processor or bank settlement totals to the books and prepare proposed reclasses or correcting entries. Output matched payments, exceptions, unapplied cash, fees/adjustments, and unresolved items.
