# Budgeting

Create a driver-based budget tied to the chart of accounts and operating assumptions.

## Workflow
1. Establish planning horizon, entities, departments, and reporting cadence.
2. Load prior-year actuals and current-year run rate.
3. Separate fixed, variable, semi-variable, payroll, capex, debt, and one-time items.
4. Document assumptions for revenue, gross margin, headcount, compensation, occupancy, insurance, professional fees, marketing, capex, taxes, and financing.
5. Build monthly budget by account and department.
6. Check internal consistency: headcount to payroll, capex to depreciation, debt to interest, revenue to receivables/cash, purchases to inventory/AP.
7. Produce base, upside, and downside scenarios when uncertainty is material.
8. Compare actuals to budget monthly and roll significant changes into a forecast rather than rewriting historical budget.

## Output
Provide assumptions, monthly budget, annual totals, key ratios, major sensitivities, and unresolved assumptions requiring management input.

Never invent missing assumptions; flag them explicitly.
