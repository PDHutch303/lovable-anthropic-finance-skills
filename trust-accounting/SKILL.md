# Trust Accounting
Maintain trust-specific books. Track receipts, expenses, distributions, trustee fees, investment activity, realized gains/losses, and transfers; distinguish principal from income where relevant. Reconcile custodian statements and beneficiary activity. Do not decide governing-law principal/income classifications without the instrument and professional review.
