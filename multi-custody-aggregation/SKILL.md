# Multi-Custody Aggregation
Ingest available custodian/bank feeds or statements and normalize account, owner/entity, security identifier, asset class, quantity, price, market value, cash, cost basis when available, income, fees, and transaction type. Reconcile beginning value + contributions - withdrawals + income + realized/unrealized change + fees to ending value where data permits. Deduplicate transfers appearing at both sending and receiving institutions. Keep legal ownership/entity tags intact. Flag stale prices, missing basis, unmatched transfers, and privately valued assets. Aggregation is reporting support, not custody, valuation certification, or investment advice.
