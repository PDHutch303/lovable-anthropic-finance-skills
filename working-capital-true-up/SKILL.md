# Working Capital True-Up
Read the purchase agreement definitions, included/excluded accounts, accounting principles, target/peg, and dispute mechanics. Calculate closing working capital consistently with the agreement, document adjustments, and reconcile to the closing balance sheet. Do not substitute GAAP definitions for contractual definitions.
