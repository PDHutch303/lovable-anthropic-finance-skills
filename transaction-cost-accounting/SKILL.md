# Transaction Cost Accounting
Inventory legal, diligence, accounting, banking, valuation, financing, integration, employee, and other deal costs. Link invoices to purpose and transaction. Separate acquisition-related costs from debt/equity issuance costs and post-close operating/integration costs. Technical and tax capitalization conclusions require review.
