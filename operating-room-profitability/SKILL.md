# Operating Room Profitability
Measure room hours available/used, cases, turnover time, revenue, direct clinical labor, anesthesia/facility costs, implants/supplies, cleaning/sterilization, equipment, and contribution per room hour. Separate fixed facility cost from incremental procedure cost.
