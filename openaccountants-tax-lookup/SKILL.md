# OpenAccountants Tax Lookup
When the OpenAccountants MCP is configured, use it for jurisdiction-specific filing, nexus, payroll, entity-tax, VAT/GST/sales-tax, and compliance research. Record jurisdiction, tax type, effective date, source title, and retrieved rule. Never claim a rule is CPA-verified or current unless the connected source actually returned that evidence. If the MCP is unavailable, say so and use current authoritative government/primary sources instead when research is required. Separate research findings from the client-specific tax conclusion and identify facts that a qualified tax professional must validate.
