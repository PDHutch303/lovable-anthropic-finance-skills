# Payroll Reconciliation

Confirm pay period, pay date, entity, payroll provider, and funding accounts. Tie gross wages, employer taxes, employee withholdings, benefits, deductions, reimbursements, and net pay to the payroll register and GL. Reconcile payroll clearing and liability accounts to payments and filings. Identify timing differences, duplicate payroll entries, stale liabilities, employee advances, and coding issues by department/class/location. Output reconciliation summary, differences, proposed entries, and unresolved payroll-tax or benefit items requiring specialist review.
