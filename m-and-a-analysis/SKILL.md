# M&A Analysis
Evaluate acquisitions from strategic, financial, and cash-flow perspectives.

## Workflow
1. Define strategic rationale and target criteria.
2. Normalize EBITDA and working capital; identify debt-like and cash-like items.
3. Build purchase-price and sources-and-uses schedules.
4. Model financing, synergies, integration costs, tax assumptions, and post-close liquidity.
5. Calculate accretion/dilution, leverage, DSCR, return on invested capital, IRR, and payback where relevant.
6. Run downside cases and identify diligence gaps.
7. Create a closing-to-integration KPI plan.

## Output
Provide valuation, normalized earnings, sources/uses, financing, returns, risks, and diligence priorities.

## Guardrails
Do not treat preliminary diligence as a legal, tax, quality-of-earnings, or fairness opinion.
