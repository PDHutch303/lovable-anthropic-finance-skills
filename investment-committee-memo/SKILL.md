# Investment Committee Memo

Follow `QUALITY-STANDARD.md`.

The memo should make the evidence, underwriting assumptions, downside, unresolved diligence and decision conditions visible. It is not a sales deck for the deal.

## Source set

Pin deal/version/date, target/entity, seller/sponsor, transaction structure, financial model version, QoE version, diligence sources, management data, legal/tax/technical workstreams and open-item list. Material figures should trace to the model or cited source rather than being retyped independently.

## Recommended structure

### 1. Decision requested
State transaction, proposed investment, structure, ownership, key valuation/leverage terms, timing and exact approval/condition requested.

### 2. Investment thesis
Use a small number of evidence-backed reasons the investment may create durable value. Distinguish current facts from future execution assumptions.

### 3. Business / market
Business model, customers, products/services, market position, competitive dynamics, cyclicality and key external dependencies.

### 4. Financial quality
Historical revenue/EBITDA/cash flow, QoE bridge, working capital, capex, concentration, revenue quality, recurring/nonrecurring items and accounting/data limitations.

### 5. Transaction and valuation
Enterprise/equity value bridge, purchase multiple, sources/uses, rollover/seller financing/earnout and relevant valuation cross-checks.

### 6. Financing and downside resilience
Debt tranches, leverage, interest/cash burden, covenants/headroom, liquidity, downside case and refinancing/maturity exposure.

### 7. Returns
Base/upside/downside sponsor return outputs with IRR/MOIC assumptions, exit bridge and major return drivers. Link to the controlled model rather than manually recomputing headline returns.

### 8. Value-creation plan
Concrete initiatives, owner, timing, investment/capex need, expected operational KPI and financial bridge. Targets are underwriting assumptions until proven.

### 9. Risks and mitigants
Rank material commercial, customer, operational, management, financial, legal, tax, regulatory, technology, integration and execution risks. A mitigant should be an actual control/condition/action, not a restatement that management is confident.

### 10. Diligence status / open items
Show unresolved diligence, expected resolution, owner, deadline and whether it is a pre-close condition, purchase-price issue, documentation item, monitoring item or deal blocker.

### 11. Recommendation / conditions
State the decision options and any approval conditions. If critical diligence is unresolved, do not convert uncertainty into an unconditional recommendation.

## Internal consistency checks

- transaction values agree with the current model;
- QoE/EBITDA definition is consistent across valuation, leverage and returns;
- sources/uses and ownership reconcile;
- debt/leverage figures match financing assumptions;
- returns match model version/date;
- downside assumptions are internally coherent;
- material risks/open diligence appearing in source work are not omitted from the memo.

## Output

Produce a concise decision document with tables where numbers matter, explicit source/model references, assumptions, conditions and an appendix only for supporting detail that would interrupt the decision narrative.

## Guardrails

Do not present management forecasts, synergies, multiple expansion or exit assumptions as facts. Do not suppress a known material diligence issue because it weakens the thesis. Investment decisions remain subject to the organization's authorized decision-makers.
