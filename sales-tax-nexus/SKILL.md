# Sales Tax Nexus & Compliance Support

Use this skill to organize sales-tax facts for review; do not make legal conclusions without current jurisdiction-specific authority.

## Workflow
1. Identify selling entity, customer ship-to/service location, product/service type, transaction date, and sales amount.
2. Summarize physical and economic nexus indicators by state/local jurisdiction.
3. Separate taxable, exempt, resale, marketplace-facilitated, and out-of-scope sales.
4. Track registrations, filing frequencies, collected tax, remitted tax, and open notices.
5. Reconcile sales-tax liability accounts to filed returns and payments.
6. Flag threshold proximity, missing exemption certificates, unregistered jurisdictions, rate anomalies, and stale liabilities for tax-professional review.

## Output
Provide jurisdiction summary, sales totals, collected/remitted tax, registration status, exception list, and questions requiring current tax-law confirmation.
