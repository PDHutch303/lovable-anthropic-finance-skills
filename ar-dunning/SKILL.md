# AR Dunning

Create a disciplined collection cadence that protects cash flow while preserving customer relationships.

## Workflow
1. Segment open invoices by aging bucket, amount, customer, and dispute status.
2. Exclude invoices with unresolved billing errors until ownership is assigned.
3. Prioritize high-dollar, oldest, high-risk, and strategically important accounts.
4. Use escalating contact cadence: reminder, direct follow-up, management escalation, payment plan, credit hold, and legal escalation when appropriate.
5. Track promise-to-pay dates and broken promises separately.
6. Reconcile customer responses back to the AR subledger before closing collection items.
7. Coordinate credit memos, unapplied cash, short pays, and disputes with accounting.

## Output
Provide a prioritized action list with customer, invoice, balance, days outstanding, status, next action, owner, and target date. Draft collection messages when requested, but do not threaten legal action unless management has authorized that step.
