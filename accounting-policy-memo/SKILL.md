# Accounting Policy Memo
Structure: purpose, scope, background/facts, definitions, authoritative basis, policy election, accounting treatment, journal-entry examples, presentation/disclosure, controls, documentation requirements, effective date, ownership, and review cadence.

## Quality Checks
Ensure consistency with actual operations, chart of accounts, close process, and financial statements. Identify judgment areas and exceptions requiring controller/CPA approval.
