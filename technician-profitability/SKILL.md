# Technician Profitability
For each technician calculate revenue, jobs, average ticket, billable/sold hours, productive hours, direct wage/burden, materials, commissions, callbacks, and contribution margin. Normalize for role, territory, season, and job mix. Do not use financial metrics alone for employment decisions.
