# Fleet / Truck Costing
Roll up lease/depreciation, fuel, maintenance/repairs, tires, insurance, registration, telematics, accidents, and downtime. Calculate monthly and per-mile/per-job/per-technician cost. Separate avoidable operating cost from sunk/fixed fleet cost for replacement decisions.
