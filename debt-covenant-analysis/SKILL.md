# Debt & Covenant Analysis

Evaluate debt capacity and covenant compliance using the governing debt documents and reconciled financial data.

## Workflow
1. List each facility: lender, commitment, outstanding balance, rate, maturity, amortization, collateral, guarantees, and covenants.
2. Extract covenant definitions exactly from the loan documents; do not substitute generic formulas when defined terms differ.
3. Calculate leverage, DSCR, fixed-charge coverage, minimum liquidity, borrowing base, and other applicable tests.
4. Reconcile EBITDA or adjusted EBITDA to the covenant definition, documenting permitted add-backs.
5. Forecast covenant compliance under base and downside scenarios through the next 12 months or maturity.
6. Identify headroom, cure rights, reporting deadlines, renewal dates, and refinancing needs.
7. Flag cross-default, change-of-control, distribution, capex, acquisition, and additional-debt restrictions where relevant.

## Output
Provide covenant calculation, threshold, actual/forecast result, headroom, compliance status, source definition, and recommended action. Never state legal compliance if the governing documents or required data have not been reviewed.
