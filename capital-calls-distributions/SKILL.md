# Capital Calls & Distributions
For each notice, identify entity, fund, commitment, funded-to-date, unfunded balance, due date, amount, purpose, bank account, and supporting notice. Reconcile cash movement to the investment ledger and distinguish return of capital, income, realized proceeds, fees, and recallable distributions when known.

## Output
Provide funding calendar, commitment roll-forward, proposed accounting treatment, missing documentation, and liquidity impact.

## Guardrails
Do not infer tax character from cash-distribution labels alone.
