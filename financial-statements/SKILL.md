# Financial Statements

Use this skill to turn trial-balance or ledger data into management-ready financial statements. Financial statements should be reviewed by a qualified accounting professional before external use.

## Workflow
1. Confirm entity, reporting period, accounting basis, currency, and comparison periods.
2. Validate that the trial balance balances and that subledgers and material balance-sheet accounts are reconciled.
3. Map accounts into financial-statement groupings consistently across periods.
4. Prepare P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement as requested.
5. Calculate dollar and percentage variances versus prior period, prior year, budget, or forecast.
6. Flag material or unusual movements and identify likely drivers.
7. Check retained earnings, cash movement, debt, fixed assets, intercompany balances, and current/non-current classification for consistency.

## Presentation
Show subtotals such as revenue, gross profit, operating income, EBITDA when appropriate, net income, current assets/liabilities, total assets/liabilities/equity, and operating/investing/financing cash flows.

## Output
Provide the statements, key ratios or margins, material variances, unresolved accounting questions, and any recommended adjusting entries or follow-up schedules.
