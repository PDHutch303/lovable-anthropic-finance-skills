# Contract to Invoice
1. Identify the governing executed document and customer/entity.
2. Extract rates, fixed fees, milestones, reimbursables, retainers, deposits, caps, taxes, billing cadence, payment terms, and change orders.
3. Compare every invoice line to contractual support and underlying time/usage/delivery evidence.
4. Flag overbilling, underbilling, duplicate billing, unsupported fees, expired terms, missing change orders, and timing/cutoff issues.
5. Produce a tie-out: contract term → billing basis → invoice amount → exception/action.
Never infer a contractual right from prior billing practice when the executed agreement conflicts or is missing.
