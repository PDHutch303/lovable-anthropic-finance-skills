# Working Capital

Analyze AR days, inventory days, AP days, cash-conversion cycle, overdue balances, slow-moving inventory, vendor terms, and seasonal trends. Separate sustainable operating improvements from one-time payment delays or collection pushes. Identify cash trapped in receivables, inventory, deposits, prepaids, and excess operating balances. Quantify the cash impact of changing DSO, DIO, or DPO assumptions. Output current metrics, major drivers, opportunities, risks, and practical actions with estimated cash impact.
