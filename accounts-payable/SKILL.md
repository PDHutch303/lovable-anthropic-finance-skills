# Accounts Payable

Review invoices for vendor, entity, invoice number/date, due date, amount, PO/support, coding, tax, approval, and duplicate risk. Distinguish expense, inventory, fixed asset, prepaid, and intercompany items. Flag invoices without required approval or support. At month-end, identify received-but-unrecorded liabilities and cutoff issues. Output invoices ready to pay, blocked invoices, duplicate risks, accrual candidates, and aging by due status.
