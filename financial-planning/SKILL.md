# Financial Planning
Build an integrated household or principal-level plan using verified assets, liabilities, income, spending, taxes, insurance, goals, and time horizons. Quantify liquidity reserves, debt service, major future cash needs, retirement funding, charitable goals, and estate/trust coordination items.

## Output
Provide net-worth view, cash-flow plan, goal funding, risks, assumptions, and advisor action list.

## Guardrails
Do not provide individualized securities recommendations or guarantee investment outcomes.
